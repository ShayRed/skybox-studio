About this tool

Skybox generator for Metin2 and other games

Skybox Studio is a free skybox generator in your browser. You create sky, moon, sun, stars, clouds, fog and mountains on the horizon and see the result instantly in a 3D preview.

How it works

1. Pick a preset or adjust sliders and colors yourself. 2. Use "Day/night" to make the sun and moon orbit. 3. Choose size and format and export the 6 faces of the cubemap (right, left, top, bottom, front, back) as a ZIP.

DDS export

Available formats are DDS DXT1, DDS DXT5, uncompressed DDS and PNG. You can also export a whole day/night series with several times of day at once, for example for time-of-day changes in a game.
